FAQ

Before you reach out

Quick answers to what most clients ask first. Anything else — just email me.

How do you price a project?+
Most engagements are scoped as a fixed price after a discovery call, based on what's being built. For open-ended work, I can also do hourly. You'll always know the number before I start.
Do you sign NDAs?+
Yes — happy to sign an NDA before any details are shared if that's what your project needs.
Who owns the code after delivery?+
You do. Full code and IP transfer to you on final payment, with the repository and documentation handed over directly.
What's a typical project timeline?+
A single AI feature usually takes 1–2 weeks. A full product build is typically 3–6 weeks depending on scope. I'll give you a real estimate on the discovery call, not a placeholder.
How do revisions work?+
I build in stages and check in at each one, so feedback happens along the way rather than as one big revision round at the end.
Do you work with clients outside India?+
Yes — I'm remote-first and work across time zones. IST overlaps well with both European mornings and US evenings.
